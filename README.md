# Tour Guide Booking Platform

An online platform that connects **tourists with professional local tour guides** in different destinations.

The platform makes it easier for travelers to discover suitable guides, communicate with them, book tours, and manage their trips, while giving guides the tools they need to manage their services and bookings.

## 🎯 Project Overview

Finding a suitable local tour guide can be difficult when travelers need to consider factors such as destination, language, budget, activities, availability, and reviews.

This platform provides a centralized solution where tourists can **search, compare, communicate with, and book local tour guides** based on their needs.

Tour guides can create professional profiles, offer tour packages, manage their availability and bookings, communicate with tourists, and track their earnings.

## ✨ Main Features

### For Tourists

* Create an account and manage their profile
* Search for tour guides by destination
* View detailed guide profiles
* Explore tour packages
* Communicate with guides through chat
* Book available tours
* Join group tours and share costs with other travelers
* Make online payments
* Rate and review guides after completing a tour

### For Tour Guides

* Create and manage a professional profile
* Add specializations and activities
* Create and manage tour packages
* Set prices and availability
* Receive and manage booking requests
* Accept or reject bookings
* Communicate with tourists
* Manage upcoming and completed tours
* Track earnings and payments

## 👥 Target Users

**Tourists / Travelers**
People looking for local guides and tours based on their destination, budget, interests, language, availability, and ratings.

**Local Tour Guides**
Professionals who want to promote their services, manage tours and bookings, communicate with tourists, and track their earnings.

## 🗺️ Booking Flow

1. Tourist searches for a guide or tour package.
2. Tourist views the guide's profile and available options.
3. Tourist selects a date and time.
4. The system checks guide availability.
5. Tourist submits a booking request.
6. The guide accepts or rejects the request.
7. Once accepted, the booking is confirmed.
8. The tourist completes the payment.
9. After the tour, the tourist can leave a rating and review.

## 👥 Group Tours

Guides can create group tours with a maximum number of participants.

Tourists can browse available group tours, select the number of participants, and join a tour while sharing the cost with other travelers.

## 💬 Communication

The platform includes an integrated chat system that allows tourists and guides to communicate before booking and discuss tour details, requirements, and other trip information.

## ⭐ Reviews & Ratings

After completing a tour, tourists can rate and review the guide. Reviews are displayed on the guide's profile to help other travelers make better decisions.

## 💳 Payments

The platform supports online payments for confirmed bookings. Successful payments are recorded, and guides can track their payments and earnings.

The current project focuses on the core booking and management experience for tourists and tour guides.
